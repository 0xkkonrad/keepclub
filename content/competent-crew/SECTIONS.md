# Competent Crew — the 14 deck sections

Source of truth: `research/syllabus.md` (G158 logbook order, newer wording where it is
a superset). Section keys are prefixed `cc-` so they cannot collide with Day Skipper's
bare keys when the two decks live in one app.

| # | key | title | syllabus bullet (the bar) | depth |
|---|-----|-------|---------------------------|-------|

| 02 | `cc-sailhandling` | Sail handling | "Bending on, setting, reefing and handling of sails. Use of sheets and halyards and their associated winches." | DO |

## The triage rule

For every candidate Day Skipper card, ask:

> **Would a crew member be *given* this as an order, or must they *decide* it?**

- Given as an order → reusable at Competent Crew verbatim.
- Requires a decision, a calculation, a chart, an almanac, a plotter or a tide table → **cut**.
- Names an IRPCS rule number, a light character, a buoy shape or a tidal concept → **cut**.

## Expected weighting

Sections 01, 02, 03 and 13 carry the deck. Sections 09, 11 and 12 must be
**deliberately thin** — that thinness *is* the Competent Crew standard. Padding
them turns the deck into Day Skipper.

## Hard exclusions (zero cards, whatever Day Skipper has)

chartwork · DR/EP · course to steer · tides · pilotage · passage planning ·
IALA buoyage · light characteristics · IRPCS steering-and-sailing rules ·
navigation lights and day shapes · sound signals · engine maintenance ·
Mayday, Pan-Pan, DSC and distress-call format · towing · helicopter rescue ·
victualling · night cruising · berthing and anchoring as skipper · electronic
navigation · synoptic charts, fronts, isobars, Shipping Forecast interpretation.

**One exception, taken deliberately.** `cc-emergency` carries a single VHF card,
because three other cards referred to a radio the deck had never introduced. It
stops at what the set is, that channel 16 is the distress and calling channel,
and that making the call is the skipper's job. Distress procedure stays out.
