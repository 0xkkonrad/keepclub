# 13 Points of sail

## am Wind / Halbwind / raumschots / vor dem Wind {#e5d35db1ec}

**am Wind = close-hauled / on the wind** — sailing towards the wind, as close as the boat can sail efficiently.<br>
**Halbwind = beam reach** — wind from the side, roughly at right angles to the boat.<br>
**raumschots = broad reach** — wind from behind the beam.<br>
**vor dem Wind = running / dead downwind** — wind from directly astern.

## im Wind {#d8fd0cd5d3}

**Head to wind** — the bow points directly into the wind. Ordinary sails cannot drive the boat forwards in this position.
