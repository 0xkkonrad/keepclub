# 01 Bow, stern and sides

## der Bug / das Heck {#e90241f3fe}

**der Bug = bow** — the front of the boat.<br>
**das Heck = stern** — the back of the boat.

![Bow and stern](fig:hull-plan@bow,stern)

## Backbord / Steuerbord {#287d7a86d1}

**Backbord = port** — the left side when facing the bow.<br>
**Steuerbord = starboard** — the right side when facing the bow.

![Port and starboard](fig:hull-plan@port,starboard)

## mittschiffs {#3fd524f9e0}

**Amidships** — in the middle of the boat. In a helm command, **Ruder mittschiffs!** means to centre the rudder.

![Amidships](fig:hull-plan@amidships)
