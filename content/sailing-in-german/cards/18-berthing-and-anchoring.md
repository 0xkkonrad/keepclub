# 18 Berthing and anchoring

## anlegen {#f80ba69cfb}

**Berth / come alongside** — bring the boat to a quay, pontoon or other berth.

## ablegen {#3182d44f9f}

**Leave the berth / cast off** — depart from a quay, pontoon or other berth.

## festmachen {#a955eb5165}

**Make fast / secure** — tie up the boat or secure a line. **Die Leine festmachen!** means “Make the line fast!”

## der Anker {#91db78516d}

**Anchor** — the equipment lowered to the seabed to hold the boat in place.

## die Ankerkette {#3841d06a91}

**Anchor chain** — the chain connecting the anchor to the boat; some boats use a combination of chain and rope.
