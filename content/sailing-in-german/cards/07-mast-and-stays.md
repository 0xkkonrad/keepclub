# 07 Mast and stays

## der Mast {#4d5f075010}

**Mast** — the upright spar supporting the sails.

![Mast](fig:sail-parts@mast)

## der Baum {#5f747e355b}

**Boom** — the spar along the foot of a sail. The mainsail's boom is also called **der Großbaum**.

![Boom](fig:sail-parts@boom)

## das Vorstag {#d60b668d74}

**Forestay** — the standing-rigging wire or rod supporting the mast towards the bow.

![Forestay](fig:rigging@forestay)

## das Achterstag {#0969f5b69c}

**Backstay** — the standing-rigging wire or rod supporting the mast towards the stern.

![Backstay](fig:rigging@backstay)

## die Wanten {#ed8c84f696}

**Shrouds** — the rigging that supports the mast from the sides. **Die Wanten** is plural; one shroud is **die Want**.

![Shrouds](fig:rigging@shrouds)
