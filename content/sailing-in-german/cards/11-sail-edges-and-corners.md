# 11 Sail edges and corners

## das Vorliek / das Achterliek / das Unterliek {#e6aad46196}

On a triangular sail:<br>
**das Vorliek = luff** — the leading edge.<br>
**das Achterliek = leech** — the trailing edge.<br>
**das Unterliek = foot** — the bottom edge.

![Sail edges](fig:sail-parts@luff,leech,foot)

## der Segelkopf / der Hals / das Schothorn {#fcd87b0f0a}

On a triangular sail:<br>
**der Segelkopf = head** — the top corner.<br>
**der Hals = tack** — the lower forward corner.<br>
**das Schothorn = clew** — the lower aft corner.

![Sail corners](fig:sail-parts@head,tack,clew)
