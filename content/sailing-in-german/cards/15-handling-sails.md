# 15 Handling sails

## die Segel setzen {#a69a302a2d}

**Set / hoist the sails** — raise or deploy them for sailing. **Großsegel setzen!** means “Hoist the mainsail!”

## die Segel bergen {#ecce91cdfa}

**Take in / lower the sails** — bring them down or otherwise take them out of use. **Bergen** is broader than just pulling a sail down.

## reffen {#2dd69fa519}

**Reef** — reduce the area of sail set. **Das Großsegel reffen** means “reef the mainsail”.

## dichtholen {#7447c6d447}

**Haul in / sheet in** — pull a line in. **Die Schot dichtholen** means “sheet in”.

## fieren {#a85eaaf364}

**Ease / pay out** — let a line out under control. **Die Schot fieren** means “ease the sheet”.
