# 02 Directions around the boat

## voraus {#2f5544e458}

**Ahead** — in front of the boat. **Fahrt voraus** means moving forwards.

## achteraus {#979a4cc592}

**Astern** — behind the boat. **Fahrt achteraus** means moving backwards.

## querab {#7955561190}

**Abeam** — off either side, at a right angle to the boat's fore-and-aft line.

## an Bord {#ca5bc7403b}

**Aboard / on board** — on or into the boat. **Komm an Bord!** means “Come aboard!”
