# 19 Course, distance and speed

## der Kompass {#997183d01a}

**Compass** — an instrument showing direction relative to north.

## der Kurs {#296b8950fb}

**Course** — the direction the boat is steered or travels, usually expressed in degrees. More precise terms distinguish a steered course from a course over ground.

## die Seemeile {#f9375f5a80}

**Nautical mile** — a unit of distance equal to **1,852 metres**.

## der Knoten (Geschwindigkeit) {#680cbfba0b}

**Knot** — a unit of speed equal to **one nautical mile per hour**. **Knoten** can also mean a knot tied in a rope.

## die Flaute {#57ffb3fd7d}

**Calm** — a period of little or no wind.
