# 09 Sail trim and hardware

## der Baumniederholer {#cfaf3bb2b8}

**Boom vang** (UK: **kicker**) — the tackle or strut that restrains the boom from lifting.

![Boom vang](fig:rigging@kicker)

## der Unterliekstrecker {#43c1fd41d6}

**Outhaul** — the control that pulls the mainsail's clew along the boom to adjust tension in the foot.

## die Winsch {#2cb599651f}

**Winch** — a drum used to gain pulling power when handling a loaded line.

## die Winschkurbel {#084efae95f}

**Winch handle** — the removable handle used to turn a winch.

## der Block {#cb292ab232}

**Block** — a pulley in a housing, used to lead a line or form part of a tackle.
