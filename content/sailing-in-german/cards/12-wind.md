# 12 Wind around the boat

## Luv / Lee {#6b75599557}

**Luv = windward** — towards the side the wind comes from.<br>
**Lee = leeward** — towards the side away from the wind.

![Windward and leeward](fig:windward-lee@wind,windward,leeward)

## der wahre Wind {#cf0172c8fd}

**True wind** — the wind before accounting for the boat's motion. The wind felt on a moving boat is the apparent wind.

## der scheinbare Wind {#1305dc7a8e}

**Apparent wind** — the wind felt on board a moving boat, combining the true wind with the effect of the boat's motion.

## die Bö {#a8ab2774d7}

**Gust** — a brief increase in wind speed. Also written **die Böe**; the plural is **die Böen**.
