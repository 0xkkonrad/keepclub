# 10 Sails and battens

## das Großsegel {#0bacef3b0d}

**Mainsail** — the principal sail set behind the main mast.

## die Fock {#e65bd7e719}

**Jib** — on a typical modern yacht, a headsail that does not overlap the mast.

## die Genua {#5a6633fc50}

**Genoa** — an overlapping headsail, extending aft beyond the mast.

## der Spinnaker {#19fe6e5fea}

**Spinnaker** — a large, light sail used mainly with the wind from the side or behind.

## die Segellatte {#bcfc0f41e6}

**Sail batten** — a stiff strip fitted into a sail pocket to support the sail's shape.
