# 05 Deck and openings

## das Deck {#608c09e7a4}

**Deck** — the boat's upper walking surface. **An Deck** means “on deck”.

## das Cockpit {#f4e6795142}

**Cockpit** — the recessed area where the crew usually sits and steers. Also called **die Plicht** in German.

## der Niedergang {#0dc6bbfd8a}

**Companionway** — the entrance and steps leading below deck.

## die Luke {#d067d153ab}

**Hatch** — an opening in the deck or cabin top, usually with a closable cover.

## die Backskiste {#f56f4faa94}

**Cockpit locker / seat locker** — a storage compartment, often beneath a cockpit seat.
