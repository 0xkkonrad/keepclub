# 03 Hull and steering

## der Rumpf {#ad78c0c3fc}

**Hull** — the main body of the boat.

## der Kiel {#cb8f07ceb2}

**Keel** — on a typical sailing yacht, the fixed fin beneath the hull that resists sideways drift and usually carries ballast.

## das Ruder {#8a43f43948}

**Rudder** — the underwater steering surface. In commands, **Ruder** can also refer to the helm; it does not mean the wheel alone.

## die Pinne {#d86879dd55}

**Tiller** — the lever used to turn the rudder.

## das Steuerrad {#1b819ffb97}

**Steering wheel / wheel** — the wheel used to steer the boat.
