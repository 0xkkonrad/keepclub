# 17 Mooring lines and fenders

## der Festmacher {#d77f6a6bce}

**Mooring line / mooring warp** — a line used to secure a boat to a berth or mooring.

## die Vorleine / die Achterleine {#aed308e0c6}

**die Vorleine = bow line** — a mooring line leading forward from the bow.<br>
**die Achterleine = stern line** — a mooring line leading aft from the stern.

![Bow and stern lines](fig:mooring-lines@bow-line,stern-line)

## die Vorspring / die Achterspring {#258983bf4c}

**die Vorspring = forward spring / bow spring** — leads aft from the forward part of the boat and checks forward movement.<br>
**die Achterspring = aft spring / stern spring** — leads forward from the aft part of the boat and checks backward movement.

English spring-line names vary; the lead and the movement restrained are the useful distinction.

![Spring lines](fig:mooring-lines@fore-spring,back-spring)

## der Fender {#eb94cd2fcb}

**Fender** — a cushion hung between the hull and a quay, pontoon or another boat to prevent contact damage.

![Fenders](fig:mooring-lines@fenders)
