# 04 The hull in the water

## die Wasserlinie {#bed24d1f76}

**Waterline** — the line where the water surface meets the hull.

![Waterline](fig:hull-profile@waterline)

## der Tiefgang {#b844d2633a}

**Draught** (US: **draft**) — the vertical distance from the waterline to the boat's lowest underwater point.

![Draught](fig:hull-profile@draught,waterline)

## der Freibord {#d553c9df09}

**Freeboard** — the vertical distance from the waterline to the deck edge.

![Freeboard](fig:hull-profile@freeboard,waterline)

## die Krängung {#61114a3d8e}

**Heel / heeling** — the boat leaning to one side, for example under wind pressure. The verb is **krängen**.

## die Bilge {#1340bb654a}

**Bilge** — the lowest internal part of the hull, where water can collect.
