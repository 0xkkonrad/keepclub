# 16 Lines and knots

## die Leine {#83d53ef966}

**Line / rope** — a length of rope used on board. Lines often have more specific names according to their job.

## der Palstek {#9c8150595d}

**Bowline** — a knot forming a fixed loop. Here, **bowline** is the knot; a **bow line** is a mooring line at the bow.

## der Webeleinstek {#1908c71f39}

**Clove hitch** — a hitch around a post or rail, commonly used to attach a fender line.

## der Achtknoten {#cca57a99c7}

**Figure-of-eight knot** — a stopper knot tied near the end of a line.

## eine Leine aufschießen {#f25621bc29}

**Coil a line** — gather it into orderly loops for stowage.
