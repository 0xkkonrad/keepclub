# 06 Rails and cleats

## die Reling / die Relingsstütze {#fbcfbf5d68}

**die Reling = guardrail / railing** — the barrier along the deck edge; its wires are often called **lifelines** in US English.<br>
**die Relingsstütze = stanchion** — an upright post supporting it.

![Guardrails and stanchions](fig:deck-fittings@guardrails,stanchions)

## der Bugkorb {#ea99fb2db0}

**Pulpit / bow rail** — the rigid safety rail at the bow.

![Pulpit](fig:deck-fittings@pulpit)

## der Heckkorb {#08dfc889c8}

**Pushpit / stern rail** — the rigid safety rail at the stern.

![Pushpit](fig:deck-fittings@pushpit)

## die Klampe {#c77f1e709b}

**Cleat** — a fitting with two horns around which a line can be secured.

![Cleat](fig:deck-fittings@cleat)
