# 20 Personal safety

## die Rettungsweste {#6c7e149ec5}

**Lifejacket** — personal flotation equipment designed to support a person in the water. **Rettungsweste anlegen!** means “Put on your lifejacket!”

## der Rettungsring {#2cb1122452}

**Lifebuoy / life ring** — a floating ring thrown to someone in the water.

## der Lifebelt {#4160adfe72}

**Safety harness** — the harness worn to connect a person to the boat using a tether. On modern lifejackets the harness may be integrated.

## Mann über Bord! {#6ab1bdec83}

**Man overboard! / Person overboard!** — the alarm that someone has fallen into the water. **Mensch über Bord!** is also used in German.
