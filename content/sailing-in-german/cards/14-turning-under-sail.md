# 14 Turning under sail

## anluven {#d0a24fa730}

**Head up / luff up** — turn the bow towards the wind.

## abfallen {#b9692b02ca}

**Bear away / bear off** — turn the bow away from the wind.

## wenden {#41f21e8a58}

**Tack / go about** — change tack by turning the bow through the wind. **Klar zur Wende!** means “Ready about!”

## halsen {#c8a0248bab}

**Gybe** (US: **jibe**) — change tack by turning the stern through the wind. **Klar zur Halse!** means “Stand by to gybe!”

## kreuzen {#eee20ad733}

**Beat to windward** — work upwind by sailing successive tacks.
