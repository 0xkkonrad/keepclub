# 08 Sail-control lines

## das Fall {#53972dcd54}

**Halyard** — a line used to hoist a sail. Nautical German uses **das Fall**; the plural is **die Fallen**.

![Halyard](fig:rigging@halyard)

## die Schot {#9ed1f79557}

**Sheet** — a line used to adjust a sail's angle to the wind.

![Sheet](fig:rigging@sheet)

## die Großschot {#824261ed2b}

**Mainsheet** — the sheet controlling the mainsail, usually through its boom.

## die Fockschot {#808aaf2d02}

**Jib sheet** — the sheet controlling the jib.

## die Dirk {#2f0e5cfcb3}

**Topping lift** — a line from high on the mast to the boom's outer end, used to support the boom.

![Topping lift](fig:rigging@topping-lift)
