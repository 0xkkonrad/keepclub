# 19 Basic qualities

## What does "pona" mean?

Good, simple, nice, or well &mdash; Toki Pona's single most-used word, and, as a verb with <b>e</b>, "to fix" or "to improve." <b>o pona e ni</b> is "please fix this."

## What does "ike" mean?

Bad, negative, excessive, or unpleasant &mdash; the natural opposite of <b>pona</b>, covering everything from "not great" to "genuinely harmful" depending on context and tone.

## What does "suli" mean?

Big, large, tall, or important &mdash; size and significance share the same word, the way English sometimes says "a big deal."

## What does "lili" mean?

Small, little, or young &mdash; the opposite of <b>suli</b> in both senses, covering physical smallness and being minor/young.

## What does "sin" mean?

New, fresh, additional, or another &mdash; something just arrived or added. <b>tenpo sin</b>, "new time," is one way to say "a new era" or mark a fresh start.

## What does "ante" mean?

Different, other, or changed &mdash; and, as a verb, "to change." <b>ni li ante</b> is "this is different"; <b>mi ante e ni</b> is "I change this."

## Translate into Toki Pona: "This tool is small."

<b>ilo ni li lili.</b>
