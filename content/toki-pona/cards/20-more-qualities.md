# 20 More qualities

## What does "wawa" mean?

Strong, powerful, or energetic &mdash; and, as a noun, strength, energy, or power itself. <b>jan wawa</b> is "a strong/powerful person."

## What does "nasa" mean?

Strange, weird, silly, or crazy &mdash; and, informally, drunk or intoxicated. <b>ni li nasa</b> is "this is weird"; the same word covers "unusual" and "under the influence" depending on context.

## What does "jaki" mean?

Dirty, gross, unsanitary, or taboo &mdash; anything that provokes disgust or is considered off-limits. <b>telo jaki</b> is "dirty water."

## What does "suwi" mean?

Sweet, or cute/adorable &mdash; both the taste and the charm sense, the same overlap English sometimes makes ("sweet" as in candy or as in kind).

## What does "sewi" mean?

Up, above, high, or elevated &mdash; and, by extension, sacred or divine, the way "the heavens above" carries both a direction and a reverence. <b>mun li lon sewi</b> is "the moon is up above."

## What does "anpa" mean?

Down, below, or low &mdash; the opposite of <b>sewi</b> &mdash; and, by extension, humble or subordinate. As a verb it can mean "to defeat" or "to lower/humiliate."

## What does "namako" mean?

Spice, seasoning, or garnish &mdash; and more generally, something extra or additional layered onto a base. <b>moku namako</b> is "spiced/seasoned food."
