# 21 Colors

## What does "kule" mean?

Color, or colorful/multicolored in general &mdash; the umbrella word before any specific hue is named. In modern community use, <b>kule</b> alone (without a specific color after it) has also become a common way to describe queer identity.

## What does "loje" mean?

Red.

## What does "jelo" mean?

Yellow &mdash; and, loosely, colors in the yellow-to-orange-to-brown range that don't have their own dedicated word.

## What does "laso" mean?

Blue &mdash; and, since Toki Pona doesn't have a separate word for green, also green and blue-green. Which shade is meant comes from context or from adding a modifier.

## What does "walo" mean?

White, pale, or light-colored &mdash; covering not just white itself but light shades in general, the way <b>pimeja</b> covers dark ones.

## What does "pimeja" mean?

Black, dark, or dark-colored &mdash; the counterpart to <b>walo</b>, covering darkness and dark shades broadly rather than only literal black.
