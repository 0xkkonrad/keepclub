# 12 Words and symbols

## What does "nimi" mean?

Word or name &mdash; any unit of language, spoken or written, including what something or someone is called. <b>nimi mi</b> is "my name"; <b>nimi ni li seme?</b> is "what does this word mean?"

## What does "sitelen" mean?

Image, picture, symbol, or writing &mdash; and, as a verb, to draw or write. <b>mi sitelen e ni</b> is "I draw/write this." It's also the root of <b>sitelen pona</b>, Toki Pona's optional pictogram script.

## What does "lipu" mean?

A flat, thin thing &mdash; paper, a book, a card, a document, or (by extension in modern use) a web page. <b>lipu mi</b> could be "my book" or "my document," depending on what's actually flat and holding the words.

## What does "kalama" mean?

Sound or noise in general, and to make one. <b>kalama li mute</b> is "there's a lot of noise."

## What does "mu" mean?

The sound an animal makes &mdash; a moo, woof, meow, or similar &mdash; used both to describe such a sound and, playfully, as an interjection imitating one, the way "woof!" can stand alone in English.

## What does "pu" mean?

Technically, to interact with the official Toki Pona book, as in reading, studying, or using it. Speakers also use <b>pu</b> informally for the book itself, the variety it teaches, or the idea of acting "by the book," but those are extensions of the technical person&ndash;book relationship.

## Translate into Toki Pona: "I write this word."

<b>mi sitelen e nimi ni.</b>
