# 09 People and family

## What does "jan" mean?

Person, human being, or somebody. It's also the word placed before a personal name: <b>jan Ana</b> is "the person named Ana," which is how Toki Pona introduces proper names into an otherwise tiny vocabulary.

## What does "mi" mean?

"I," "me," or "we/us" &mdash; Toki Pona doesn't distinguish singular from plural, so <b>mi</b> alone means "I" and <b>mi mute</b> ("I many") specifies "we." It's one of the two subjects that can drop <b>li</b>.

## What does "sina" mean?

"You," singular or plural, with the same non-distinction as <b>mi</b> &mdash; <b>sina</b> can mean one listener or several. It's the other subject allowed to drop <b>li</b>.

## What does "ona" mean?

"He," "she," "it," or "they" &mdash; Toki Pona's one third-person pronoun, covering every gender and number English splits apart. Which one is meant comes entirely from context.

## What does "meli" mean?

Woman, girl, or something feminine &mdash; as a modifier it marks the feminine version of a more general word, the way <b>jan meli</b> narrows "person" to "woman."

## What does "mije" mean?

Man, boy, or something masculine, the counterpart to <b>meli</b>: <b>jan mije</b> is "man," narrowing <b>jan</b> the same way.

## What does "mama" mean?

Parent, ancestor, or caretaker &mdash; a broad family-and-origin word rather than specifically "mother" or "father." <b>mama meli</b> and <b>mama mije</b> narrow it to mother and father when the distinction matters.

## What does "kulupu" mean?

Group, community, organization, or nation &mdash; any collection of people (or things) treated as one unit. <b>kulupu mi</b> can be "my group," "my team," or "my country," depending on scale.

## What does "olin" mean?

To love, or love itself &mdash; deep affection or care, romantic or otherwise. <b>mi olin e sina</b> is "I love you"; as a noun it names the feeling directly.
