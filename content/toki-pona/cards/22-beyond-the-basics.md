# 22 Beyond the basics

## What does "a" mean?

Nothing on its own but emphasis or emotion &mdash; an interjection like "ah," "oh," "wow," or "ha," standing alone or tacked onto a sentence to color its tone. <b>pona a!</b> is "good, wow!" or "great!"

## What does "mani" mean?

Money, currency, or wealth &mdash; and, in some contexts, valuable livestock, echoing how many real languages' words for money trace back to cattle as an early store of value.

## This course has covered roughly 120 core words. Is that the entire language?

It's the entire deliberately small foundation &mdash; the vocabulary this course draws from is essentially the complete <b>nimi pu</b>, the "book words" from Toki Pona's original published source. Beyond it, the community has adopted a further set of widely used words, <b>nimi ku suli</b>, for concepts pu left out (a specific word for "eye," for instance, or "mushroom" more precisely than <b>kili</b>). Fluent speakers mostly stick close to this core set rather than growing it the way natural languages do &mdash; the smallness is the point, not a gap waiting to be filled.
